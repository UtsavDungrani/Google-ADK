# BaristaPro Espresso & Cappuccino Machine - Care & Troubleshooting Manual

## 1. First-Time Setup & Water Circuit Priming
Before brewing your first cup:
1. Fill the removable water reservoir with fresh filtered water up to the `MAX` line.
2. Insert the water filter cartridge (soak in cold water for 5 minutes prior to insertion).
3. Place a 500ml container under the steam wand and another under the coffee dispenser.
4. Turn the selector knob to **Hot Water** for 30 seconds to prime the high-pressure 15-bar Italian pump.

---

## 2. Flashing Orange Indicator Light (Descaling Required)
### Automatic Alert: The orange icon indicates mineral/scale buildup.
1. Dissolve 1 packet of **BaristaPro Organic Descaler** in 1 Liter of lukewarm water in the reservoir.
2. Press and hold the **1-Cup** and **Steam** buttons together for 5 seconds to enter **Descaling Mode**.
3. The machine will pulse hot descaling solution through the group head and steam wand over a 20-minute cycle.
4. Flush the reservoir with clean water and run a full tank of pure water to complete the rinse.

---

## 3. Low Extraction Pressure / Weak Crema Troubleshooting
1. **Grind Size Adjustment**: If the pressure gauge reads below 8-9 Bars, adjust the built-in conical burr grinder to a finer setting (Settings 1-3).
2. **Tamping Pressure**: Ensure you tamp ground coffee evenly with 15-20 kg (30-40 lbs) of downward pressure.
3. **Freshness of Beans**: Coffee beans roasted more than 60 days ago lose CO2 gases and produce thin crema.

---

## 4. Grinder Jam & Bean Hopper Clearance
If the grinder motor hums but does not dispense grounds:
1. Turn off the main power switch and unplug the unit.
2. Remove the bean hopper by twisting counter-clockwise.
3. Inspect the top stainless steel burr for unroasted stones or foreign debris.
4. Use the included wire brush to clear compacted oily residue. Do NOT use water to clean internal burrs.
