# UltraHD 65" 4K Smart TV - Post-Purchase Troubleshooting & Setup Manual

## 1. Initial Setup & Wi-Fi Connection Troubleshooting
### Issue: Error Code TV-NET-502 (Cannot Connect to Wi-Fi)
1. **Power Cycle**: Unplug the TV power cord from the wall outlet for 60 seconds (do not just use the remote). Hold down the physical TV power button for 15 seconds to discharge residual power.
2. **Network Reset**: Navigate to `Settings > Network > Advanced > Reset Network Settings`.
3. **Band Selection**: Ensure you connect to the 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi band. If your router uses combined SSID (Band Steering), temporarily disable 5GHz during initial pairing.
4. **DNS Override**: Set Manual DNS to `8.8.8.8` or `1.1.1.1`.

---

## 2. HDMI eARC & Audio Sync Issues
### Issue: No Sound or Delayed Audio via Soundbar / AV Receiver
1. Connect the HDMI cable exclusively to **HDMI Port 2 (labeled eARC/ARC)**. Standard HDMI ports do not support bi-directional audio return.
2. Navigate to `Settings > Sound > Expert Settings > Digital Output Audio Format` and select **Pass-Through** or **Bitstream** instead of PCM.
3. Enable `Settings > General > External Device Manager > Anynet+ (HDMI-CEC)`.

---

## 3. Display Flickering & Black Screen Calibration
1. **Self-Diagnostic Test**: Go to `Settings > Support > Device Care > Self Diagnosis > Picture Test`.
2. If flickering persists only on external inputs (e.g. Gaming Console, Apple TV), replace the cable with a certified **Ultra High Speed HDMI 2.1 Cable (48Gbps)**.
3. Disable `Settings > General > Eco Solution > Ambient Light Detection` to prevent unexpected brightness fluctuations.

---

## 4. Factory Reset Procedure
If the operating system becomes unresponsive:
1. Navigate to `Settings > General > Reset`.
2. Enter the default master PIN: `0000` (or `1111` if reset previously).
3. Select **Factory Data Reset**. The TV will reboot in 2 minutes.
