# ProSound ANC Wireless Headphones - User Guide & Troubleshooting

## 1. Bluetooth Multipoint Pairing & Reconnection
### How to pair with two devices simultaneously:
1. Turn off Bluetooth on your primary device.
2. Put the headphones into Pairing Mode by holding the Power button for **7 seconds** until the LED indicator pulses blue/red.
3. Select `ProSound-ANC-900` on your secondary device (e.g. Laptop).
4. Re-enable Bluetooth on your primary device (e.g. Phone) and select `ProSound-ANC-900`. The voice prompt will confirm: *"Two devices connected"*.

---

## 2. Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) & Ambient Mode
- **Switch Modes**: Tap the touch sensor on the **Right Ear Cup** for 2 seconds to cycle through:
  - `ANC On`: 98% ambient low-frequency attenuation (airplane cabin, commute).
  - `Ambient Awareness`: Microphones pass through voices and traffic alerts.
  - `Off`: Passive isolation for maximum battery life (up to 45 hours).

---

## 3. Battery Drain & Charging Issues
### Issue: Headphone Not Charging or Battery Discharging Rapidly
1. Ensure you use the provided USB-C charging cable and a 5V/2A power adapter. High-wattage USB-PD laptop chargers (>65W) may trigger safety cutoff.
2. Clean the USB-C charging contacts inside the headphone port using a dry anti-static cotton swab.
3. Perform a **Hardware Battery Calibration**:
   - Charge uninterrupted to 100% for 3 hours.
   - Use until power reaches 0% and headphones shut down.
   - Re-charge to 100% continuously.

---

## 4. Audio Lag / Video Sync & Static Noise Fix
1. Enable **Low Latency Gaming/Video Mode** by triple-tapping the Left Ear Cup. Reduces audio latency from 180ms to 40ms.
2. **Hard Factory Reset**: While charging via USB-C, press and hold the **Power Button** and **Volume Down (-)** simultaneously for 10 seconds. The LED will flash violet 3 times.
