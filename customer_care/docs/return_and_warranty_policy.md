# Official Post-Purchase Return, Refund & Warranty Policy

## 1. 30-Day Hassle-Free Return Policy
- **Return Window**: Customers may return eligible products within **30 days of confirmed delivery**.
- **Condition Requirements**: Items must be in original condition with all accessories, cables, documentation, and original product packaging.
- **Prepaid Return Shipping**: We provide a prepaid digital return shipping label (FedEx / UPS / DHL) for all authorized returns.
- **Refund Processing**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **3-5 business days** after the returned package is received and inspected at our fulfillment hub.
- **Restocking Fees**: Zero restocking fees for defective items or wrong items delivered. Open-box non-defective returns incur a 0% fee if returned within the 30-day window.

---

## 2. 2-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty
- **Coverage**: All electronics and appliances come with a complimentary **2-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** against hardware defects, internal circuit failures, motor malfunctions, and screen/panel defects.
- **Exclusions**: The warranty does NOT cover accidental liquid immersion, physical drop/impact damage, unauthorized third-party modifications, or normal cosmetic wear-and-tear.
- **Express Replacement SLA**: If a product cannot be resolved through remote troubleshooting, an expedited replacement unit is dispatched within **24-48 hours** via 2-Day Air shipping.

---

## 3. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Process
To initiate an authorized return:
1. Provide the **Order ID** (e.g. `ORD-10021`) and serial number.
2. Select reason for return: `Defective / Hardware Issue`, `Missing Accessories`, `Accidental Purchase`, or `Unsatisfied with Performance`.
3. An automated RMA code (e.g. `RMA-77402`) and barcode label will be generated instantly.
4. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier drop-off kiosk.

---

## 4. Courtesy Credits & Executive Escalation
- For customers who experienced delayed deliveries (>3 days past promised SLA) or out-of-box defects, customer care specialists are empowered to issue a **$25 to $50 courtesy store credit** immediately.
- In cases of severe customer dissatisfaction, tickets are escalated to Tier-2 Human Executive Support with a guaranteed callback SLA within 2 hours.
