CloudPlatform - Billing and Subscriptions

Subscription Plans

1. Free Tier: $0/month. Up to 3 active projects, 50,000 monthly API calls, 1 GB storage. Community forum support with 48-hour response time.

2. Pro Plan: $29/month per user (or $290/year paid annually). Unlimited projects, 1,000,000 monthly API calls, 50 GB storage, custom domain SSL. Priority email support with guaranteed 8-hour response SLA.

3. Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing (contact sales@cloudplatform.example.com). Dedicated infrastructure, unlimited API calls, custom SLAs (99.99%), SSO / SAML integration, HIPAA & SOC2 compliance. Dedicated technical account manager & 24/7 phone/chat support with 15-minute response SLA.

Payment Methods & Invoicing

• We accept all major Credit/Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and ACH wire transfers for Enterprise contracts.

• Invoices are automatically generated on the 1st of each calendar month and emailed to the billing admin address.

• Invoices can be downloaded anytime from Dashboard > Settings > Billing > Invoices (PDF).

Cancellation and Refund Policy

• Cancellation: You can cancel your subscription at any time under Dashboard > Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Your plan will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

• Refund Policy: We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all first-time Pro plan purchases. If you are unsatisfied, request a refund within 14 days of purchase by submitting a support ticket.

• Enterprise contracts are governed by individual Master Services Agreements (MSAs).

Updating Payment Details

1. Log in to the CloudPlatform dashboard.

2. Navigate to Organization Settings > Billing > Payment Methods.

3. Click Add New Payment Method, enter new details, and select Set as Default.

4. Old cards can be removed by clicking the trash icon next to the card.
